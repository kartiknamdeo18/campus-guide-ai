CAMPUSGUIDE UNIVERSITY — ACADEMIC REGULATIONS & POLICIES (2026-2027)

CHAPTER 1: ATTENDANCE & LEAVE OF ABSENCE
1.1 Minimum Attendance: All students enrolled in undergraduate and postgraduate programs must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each enrolled theory and laboratory subject during the semester.
1.2 Medical Condonation: A medical condonation of up to 10% (minimum 65% aggregate attendance) may be granted by the Dean of Academic Affairs upon submission of a formal medical certificate signed by a registered medical practitioner within 5 working days of resuming classes.
1.3 Duty Leave: Students representing the university in authorized sports tournaments, cultural symposiums, or academic competitions are credited with duty attendance upon prior recommendation from the Faculty Advisor and Dean of Student Welfare.

CHAPTER 2: EXAMINATION CONDUCT & GRADING SYSTEM
2.1 Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE): CIE carries 40% weighting, comprising two mid-semester tests (20 marks), assignment submissions (10 marks), and classroom quizzes/seminars (10 marks).
2.2 Semester End Examination (SEE): SEE carries 60% weighting. A student must achieve at least 40% in SEE and 40% in aggregate (CIE + SEE) to clear the course.
2.3 Supplementary Examinations: Supplementary exams for odd and even semesters are conducted in July and January respectively. Registration fee is INR 500 per subject.
2.4 Grade Re-evaluation: Students may request answer script photocopy within 7 days of result announcement (INR 300 fee) and re-evaluation within 14 days (INR 750 fee).

CHAPTER 3: DEGREE & CREDIT ACCUMULATION
3.1 Credit Requirement: Completion of B.Tech requires 160 credits; M.Tech requires 72 credits; B.Sc requires 120 credits.
3.2 Maximum Course Duration: A 4-year degree must be completed within a maximum of 6 consecutive academic years.

CHAPTER 4: STUDENT GRIEVANCE & ANTI-RAGGING DIRECTIVES
4.1 Anti-Ragging Policy: Zero tolerance for any form of physical or mental harassment. The 24/7 Anti-Ragging Helpline is available at antiragging@campusguide.edu or Ext. 109.
4.2 Student Ombudsman: Complaints regarding academic evaluations, administrative delays, or faculty conduct may be escalated to the University Ombudsman via the online grievance cell.
